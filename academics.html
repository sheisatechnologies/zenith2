Programs in Amrit Vidya Ashram

At Amrit Vidya Ashram School, our academic framework is thoughtfully designed in alignment with the guidelines of the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and the vision of the National Education Policy 2020 (NEP 2020). We focus on experiential learning, conceptual clarity, skill development, and holistic growth across all stages of education.

Guided by our motto “Shiksha Bhi, Sanskar Bhi,” we blend academic excellence with strong values and future-ready skills.

🌼 Pre-Primary Wing (Nursery to KG)

Our early childhood program follows NEP’s foundational stage approach, emphasizing play-based and activity-driven learning. We focus on:

  • Gross and fine motor skill development
  • Phonics and early literacy
  • Foundational numeracy
  • Social and emotional growth

Learning is joyful, interactive, and developmentally appropriate.

We are proudly associated with Zenith Kids, a specialized pre-school education partner that brings structured early childhood pedagogy and modern teaching methodologies.

📘 Primary Wing (Classes I to IV)

In line with CBSE and NEP competency-based learning, the primary curriculum builds strong foundations in languages, mathematics, and environmental studies.

Along with academics, we emphasize:

  • Sports and physical education
  • Art, music, and creative expression
  • Communication skills
  • Value-based learning

Our approach ensures balanced intellectual and personal development.

🔬 Middle & Secondary (Classes V to X)

At this stage, we strengthen analytical thinking and problem-solving skills while aligning with CBSE curriculum standards. Key Highlights:

  • Introduction to AI, Robotics & Coding as part of 21st-century skill development
  • Technology-enabled and experiential classrooms
  • Strong conceptual focus in Science and Mathematics
  • Foundation Program for Classes VII–X to prepare students for future competitive exams

This structured approach ensures academic excellence while nurturing innovation and logical reasoning.

🎓 Senior Secondary (Classes XI & XII)

The senior secondary curriculum integrates CBSE board preparation with career-oriented pathways.

Science Stream

Integrated preparation for:

  • JEE / NEET / CUET
Commerce Stream

Integrated preparation for:

  • CA Foundation

By blending competitive exam readiness with school academics, students gain clarity, confidence, and stronger career prospects without additional academic burden.
